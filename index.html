Our Heritage

Goldwork of the Court

In the ateliers of the old city, goldsmiths spent lifetimes on a single craft — pierced filigree fine as lace, coin seals struck by hand, pearls carried up the Tigris from Basra.

Seraglio continues that lineage. Each piece is cast, chased and finished by hand in Istanbul, in small series, using the same techniques the court once kept behind palace walls.
